# MiDirection

Provider that sets the reading direction (LTR or RTL) for its subtree and exposes it to descendants.

Group: Base. Source: app/components/Base/MiDirection.vue

## Props

| prop | type | default | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| direction | "ltr" \| "rtl" | "ltr" | Reading direction applied to the subtree. |

## Slots

- default: Content that inherits the reading direction.
